What this event is

Participants attend a short online workshop, then spend two in-person afternoons learning startup fundamentals — identifying opportunities, the Lean Canvas, MVPs, and pitch decks — before teaming up to build and present an original business concept to a live panel of judges.

It's designed to build creativity, problem-solving, and public speaking skills, and to introduce entrepreneurship as a path — no prior DECA or business experience required.
