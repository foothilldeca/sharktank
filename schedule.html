Event schedule

Three days, start to pitch

Day 1 · Tuesday, October 5 · Online

Online workshop

Participants attend a series of short virtual lessons covering entrepreneurship fundamentals: identifying opportunities, the Lean Canvas Model, building an MVP, and structuring an effective pitch deck — taught by trained Foothill DECA mentors.

Day 2 · Wednesday, October 6 · In person, afternoon — MPR & Library

Keynote + team formation + pitch deck build

Students hear from keynote speaker Reena Gupta, are placed into teams, and spend the afternoon developing a business concept and pitch deck with support from mentors and Foothill DECA officers.

Day 3 · Thursday, October 7 · In person, afternoon — MPR

Shark Tank presentations

Teams present their pitch decks to a panel of 5–6 industry judges in a live, Shark-Tank-style competition. Awards follow the final round.
